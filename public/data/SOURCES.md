# Egypt governorate boundaries

`egypt-governorates.geojson` is the simplified ADM1 Egypt dataset from [geoBoundaries](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api.html), release commit `9469f09`, sourced from OpenStreetMap and Wambacher. License: CC BY 4.0. The map uses it to highlight governorate coverage areas; representative assignments come from the company contact page.
